## Fixes

- **Session crash when peeking other players' zones** - revealing a card from another player's graveyard or exile created a local card proxy, but that proxy was incorrectly broadcasting network events to other clients who didn't have it. Those other clients would crash trying to act on a card they'd never seen. The proxies are now marked as local-only and filtered before hitting the network.
