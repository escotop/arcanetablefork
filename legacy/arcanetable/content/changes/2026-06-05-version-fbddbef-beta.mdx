> **Staging release** — try it at [staging.arcanetable.app](https://staging.arcanetable.app/).

## Features

- **4-player table** — the table has been widened and player areas repositioned to support up to four players sitting at different sides.
- **Night sky** — the 3D scene now has a full HDR night sky environment, adding depth and atmosphere to the table.
- **Head-tilt camera** — moving your mouse across the screen now causes a subtle camera sway, giving the table a more immersive, physical feel.

## Fixes

- Fixed a rare crash in card animations caused by a missing position reference.
